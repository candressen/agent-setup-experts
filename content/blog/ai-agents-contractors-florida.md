## Real Use Cases: How Florida Contractors Are Using AI Agents

### 1. Roofing Companies — Storm Season Lead Surge

After a major storm, roofing companies in Tampa, Fort Myers, and Jacksonville can receive hundreds of inbound leads in days. Most contractors can't respond fast enough, and those leads go cold — or worse, to a competitor.

AI agents handle the surge. Every inquiry gets an immediate response, a qualification question ("Do you have visible damage from the recent storm?"), and a booking link. Your calendar fills automatically while you're assessing damage on active jobs.

### 2. HVAC Contractors — Seasonal Demand Spikes

Florida HVAC companies face brutal demand spikes in summer. Customers with broken AC units in June need help now. They'll call three companies and book the first one that responds.

AI agents answer immediately, collect key details (unit age, symptoms, address), and route urgent calls to your on-call technician while booking non-emergency appointments automatically.

### 3. General Contractors — Estimate Follow-Up

How many estimates have you sent out and never heard back from? Most contractors don't have a consistent follow-up process. An AI agent can send a follow-up text or email 24 hours after the estimate, again at 72 hours, and once more at 7 days — automatically. That alone can recover a meaningful percentage of lost bids.
