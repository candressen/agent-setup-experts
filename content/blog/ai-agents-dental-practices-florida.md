## What AI Agents Actually Do for a Dental Practice

Here's how a typical [AI agent setup](/openclaw-setup) works for a Florida dental office:

### 1. Automated Appointment Reminders
The agent sends multi-channel reminders — text, email, even voicemail — at 72 hours, 24 hours, and 2 hours before an appointment. Patients can confirm, cancel, or reschedule directly from the message. No phone tag. No missed reminders.

### 2. Smart Rescheduling
When a patient cancels, the agent instantly offers available slots and books the replacement. Your calendar fills itself. Your staff focuses on patients who are *in the chair*, not chasing ones who aren't.

### 3. Recall and Reactivation Campaigns
Patients who haven't been in for 12+ months get a personalized outreach sequence — "We miss you, it's been a while since your last cleaning." The agent handles the message, tracks replies, and routes warm leads to your front desk only when they're ready to book.

### 4. Insurance and Pre-Authorization Follow-Up
AI agents can send status-check messages to insurance carriers, track outstanding pre-auth requests, and notify patients when their coverage is confirmed. Less time on hold. Fewer delays.

### 5. Post-Visit Follow-Up and Reviews
After a procedure, the agent sends a care instructions message, checks in on recovery, and — if sentiment is positive — invites the patient to leave a Google review. Your online reputation builds automatically.
