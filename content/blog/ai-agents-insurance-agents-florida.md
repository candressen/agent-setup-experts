## What AI Agents Actually Do for Insurance Agents

AI agents are software systems that run in the background, handling repetitive communication and data tasks without you lifting a finger. For insurance agents, that looks like:

### 1. Lead Follow-Up on Autopilot

When a prospect fills out a quote form on your website or comes in through a referral, your AI agent reaches out immediately via email, SMS, or both. It introduces you, confirms their interest, and asks qualifying questions — type of coverage, current carrier, renewal date.

By the time you get involved, the lead is warm, pre-qualified, and expecting your call.

### 2. Renewal Reminders That Actually Work

Losing a client at renewal is one of the most preventable revenue leaks in insurance. AI agents can monitor your book of business, identify upcoming renewals 60-90 days out, and send a sequence of personalized touchpoints — checking in, offering a policy review, and booking a call.

No more renewals slipping through because you forgot to follow up.

### 3. Quote Status Nudges

Sent a quote and heard nothing? Your AI agent follows up at 24 hours, 72 hours, and 7 days automatically. Each message is personalized and professional. You only step in when someone responds or books a call.

### 4. Client Onboarding Sequences

After binding a policy, the real relationship-building begins. AI agents can handle welcome emails, document requests, payment setup reminders, and satisfaction check-ins — building trust while freeing you to focus on production.

### 5. Cross-Sell Campaigns

Already writing someone's auto? Your AI agent can reach out about homeowners, umbrella, or life coverage at the right time based on triggers you define. Smart, timely, not pushy.
