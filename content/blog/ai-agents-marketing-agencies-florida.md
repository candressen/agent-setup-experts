## What AI Agents Actually Do for Marketing Agencies

### 1. Automated Client Reporting

One of the biggest time sinks for any agency is assembling monthly reports. AI agents can pull data from Google Analytics, Meta Ads, Google Ads, HubSpot, and other platforms, then format and deliver a branded summary to each client on a set schedule.

No more copy-pasting numbers at midnight before a client call. The report lands in the client's inbox automatically — and your team just shows up to the conversation ready to talk strategy.

Learn more about how we set this up: [How to Automate Client Reporting with AI Agents](/automate-client-reporting-ai-agents)

### 2. Lead Follow-Up and Nurture Sequences

For agencies running lead gen campaigns for clients, AI agents can manage the initial follow-up layer — responding to new leads within minutes, qualifying them with a few questions, and routing hot prospects to the client's sales team.

This is especially powerful for Florida-based service businesses where speed-to-lead is the difference between winning and losing the deal.

### 3. Client Onboarding Automation

New client onboarding often involves dozens of back-and-forth emails to collect brand assets, access credentials, and campaign goals. An AI agent can handle this entire sequence — sending intake forms, chasing missing info, confirming access, and summarizing what was collected — all without a human touching it.

### 4. Campaign Approval Workflows

Waiting on client approvals kills momentum. AI agents can send approval requests automatically when drafts are ready, send reminders if the client goes quiet, and log approvals in your project management system — so nothing slips through.

### 5. Invoice and Payment Reminders

Chasing late invoices is uncomfortable and time-consuming. AI agents handle this gracefully — sending friendly reminders on schedule, escalating the tone if needed, and flagging overdue accounts for your ops team to follow up personally.
