Once a lead is qualified, your AI agent books the estimate directly into your calendar — no phone tag, no back-and-forth texting. It sends automated confirmations and reminders that cut no-shows by up to 40%.

The average roofing company sends an estimate and then... hopes. Your AI agent doesn't hope — it follows up. Automatically. On day 2, day 5, day 10. With the right message for each stage.

### 5. Review Collection After Job Completion

Google reviews are oxygen for local roofing companies. Your AI agent can automatically send a review request via text after job completion — personalized, timely, and frictionless for the homeowner.
