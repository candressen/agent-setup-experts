## What AI Agents Actually Do for Florida Salons and Spas

### 1. Automated Appointment Booking and Confirmation

An AI agent connected to your booking system (Vagaro, Boulevard, Mindbody, Square Appointments, etc.) can:

- Respond instantly to booking requests via text, Instagram DM, or your website chat
- Confirm, reschedule, or cancel appointments without any human involvement
- Send confirmation messages with everything the client needs: address, parking, what to bring

Clients get a fast response. You get a full calendar without lifting a finger.

### 2. Appointment Reminders That Actually Get Read

Email reminders get ignored. A well-timed text from an AI agent — sent 48 hours out and again 2 hours before — gets opened. And when a client needs to reschedule, the agent handles it immediately.

Florida salons using automated reminders typically see **no-show rates drop by 30–50%**. That is real money back in your pocket.

### 3. Re-Engagement Campaigns for Lapsed Clients

Your booking history is a goldmine. An AI agent can automatically identify clients who have not booked in 8, 12, or 16 weeks and send them a personal-feeling message:

> "Hey Sarah! It's been a while — your highlights are probably ready for a refresh. Want me to grab you a spot this week?"

No mail merge. No manual list-building. The agent runs this on a schedule, and clients respond because the message feels human, not automated.

### 4. Review Request Automation

Google reviews drive local search rankings. More reviews = more visibility = more new clients in your area. But asking for reviews in person is awkward, and most teams forget.

An AI agent sends a review request automatically 2–4 hours after a completed appointment — when the client is still glowing from their service. Click the link, leave a review, done.

This alone can **double your monthly review volume** within the first 60 days.

### 5. Retail and Product Follow-Ups

Did a client buy a keratin treatment or a new skincare product? An AI agent can follow up three days later to check in, answer questions, and suggest complementary products — turning a single visit into an ongoing relationship (and ongoing revenue).
