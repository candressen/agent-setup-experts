## What AI Agents Actually Do

An AI agent is not a chatbot. It is a system that takes actions — automatically, without someone triggering it.

For a Miami marketing agency, that means:

- **Lead follow-up**: New lead fills out a form. Agent sends a personalized email within 2 minutes, logs it to the CRM, and notifies the account manager.
- **Client reporting**: Every Monday morning, agent pulls data from Google Ads, Meta, and analytics — generates a formatted report and emails it to the client automatically.
- **Internal routing**: New project brief comes in via email. Agent categorizes it, assigns it to the right team member, and creates the task in your project management tool.
