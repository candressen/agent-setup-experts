## What AI Agents Actually Do for Real Estate Teams

An AI agent isn't just a chatbot. It's a workflow automation layer that connects your CRM, calendar, email, and communication tools — and acts on them automatically.

Here's what a properly configured AI agent handles for a Miami real estate team:

### 1. Instant Lead Response — 24/7

When a buyer submits a contact form at 11 PM, your AI agent responds immediately. It qualifies the lead (budget, timeline, property type), books a call or showing on your calendar, and logs everything to the CRM — without anyone lifting a finger.

Response time drops from hours to seconds. Lead conversion rates improve dramatically.

### 2. Automated Follow-Up Sequences

Most deals don't close on first contact. But consistent follow-up is time-consuming. AI agents send personalized follow-up messages at the right intervals — checking in after a showing, sending listing updates that match a buyer's criteria, or nudging a seller who went quiet after an initial consultation.

The follow-up never slips. Every lead gets the attention it deserves.

### 3. Transaction Coordination Automation

Once a deal is under contract, the paperwork and coordination begin. AI agents can:

- Send document checklists to buyers and sellers automatically
- Track which documents have been received and which are outstanding
- Send reminders to all parties about upcoming deadlines (inspection periods, financing contingencies, closing dates)
- Update your team's shared dashboard in real time

This alone can save a transaction coordinator 5–10 hours per deal.

### 4. Listing Marketing on Autopilot

New listing goes live? Your AI agent can automatically push updates to your CRM contacts who match the criteria, draft social media posts for your review, and schedule email blasts to your buyer list — all without manual input.

### 5. Client Communication Summaries

Agents dealing with multiple concurrent transactions often lose track of conversation threads. AI agents can summarize recent client communications, flag items requiring attention, and surface the next action needed — keeping your team in sync without the daily standup.
