## What OpenClaw Actually Does for Law Firms

OpenClaw is an AI agent platform that connects to your existing tools — your CRM, email, calendar, case management software — and runs automated workflows on your behalf. Think of it as a tireless operations assistant that never misses a follow-up.

Here's what law firms are deploying it for right now:

**1. Automated Client Intake**
When a potential client fills out a contact form or calls your office, OpenClaw can immediately send a personalized intake questionnaire, collect their responses, and route the lead to the right attorney based on practice area. No more manual triage. No more leads falling through the cracks.

**2. Appointment Reminders and Follow-Ups**
Missed appointments are expensive. OpenClaw sends automated reminders via email or SMS — and follows up if there's no confirmation. It can also reschedule on behalf of the client without pulling your front desk into the loop.

**3. Case Status Updates**
One of the most common client complaints about law firms? "They never tell me what's happening." OpenClaw can send scheduled case status updates to clients at predefined milestones — keeping them informed without requiring attorney time.

**4. Document Collection**
Need a client to send supporting documents? OpenClaw can send reminders, track receipt, and escalate to a human if the client hasn't responded after a set window. No more chasing.

**5. Post-Matter Follow-Up and Reviews**
Once a case closes, OpenClaw can automatically send a satisfaction survey and a review request — helping you build your Google and Avvo profile without lifting a finger.
