Proposed AI Agent Solutions

We recommend a phased deployment of four AI agents designed for tax and accounting workflows. Each agent supports a narrow, high-value function and includes a clear human review checkpoint.

1. Client Communication Agent

This agent handles routine communication that typically consumes front-office and preparer time.

It can send automated document reminders tailored to each client based on filing status, service type, and missing items. It can answer common questions such as “Where is my return?” by pulling the latest internal status and sending a consistent, professional update. It can also automate new client onboarding questionnaires, collect intake details, and send appointment reminders with rescheduling links.

This is especially valuable during peak filing periods, when simple client questions create a steady stream of interruptions. Instead of having staff repeat the same updates manually, the communication agent handles routine messaging in a controlled, branded format.

Human checkpoint: Any complex, sensitive, or judgment-based response is routed to a CPA or team lead for review before sending.

2. Document Collection and Intake Agent

Document collection is one of the biggest sources of delay in tax preparation. This agent creates a structured intake process so your team always knows what is missing, who has been reminded, and which files are ready for prep.

The agent tracks required documents by client, including W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, receipts, prior-year returns, bookkeeping reports, and signatures. It sends escalating reminder sequences based on due dates and urgency. As files come in, it can organize them into the correct client folders and flag incomplete submissions before a return moves into active preparation.

This reduces the back-and-forth that stretches intake across days or weeks and gives your team a cleaner starting point before work begins.

Human checkpoint: A CPA or designated staff member reviews the final document checklist before filing work starts.

3. Bookkeeping Assistance Agent

For bookkeeping and monthly close support, the opportunity is not fully automated posting. The opportunity is faster preparation for human review.

This agent extracts line items from receipts and bank statements, suggests transaction categorizations based on your chart of accounts, flags anomalies and unusual transactions, and pre-fills expense reporting data from uploaded receipts. It can also assist with reconciliation by matching transactions to statements and surfacing exceptions that need attention.

This is particularly useful for firms serving small businesses that send inconsistent source documents or mixed-quality transaction data. The agent handles the first-pass organization, while your bookkeeping team keeps final control over categorization and posting.

Human checkpoint: Your CPA or bookkeeper reviews all suggested entries before anything is posted. Nothing posts automatically.

4. Tax Prep Data Agent

This agent supports the preparation workflow by extracting structured data from uploaded documents and organizing it for review.

It can pull relevant fields from W-2s, 1099s, K-1s, mortgage interest statements, charitable receipts, and other common tax documents. It can pre-populate mapped fields for tax software workflows, cross-reference prior-year returns for consistency, and generate a concise client summary that highlights changes, missing items, and follow-up questions.

For firms handling a high volume of personal returns, this reduces manual re-entry and helps standardize prep packets before CPA review.

Human checkpoint: A CPA verifies every extracted field before the information is used in preparation or filing. The AI assists, your team reviews, and your team files.

Data Security and Compliance

Because your firm handles taxpayer data, security is not a feature. It is a requirement.

Our deployments are structured with IRS Publication 4557 in mind, along with the practical realities of handling personally identifiable information in a tax and bookkeeping environment. We design agent workflows to support the administrative, technical, and operational safeguards expected of firms that collect, store, and process taxpayer data.

IRS Publication 4557 alignment: We support implementations that fit within a Written Information Security Plan, including documented access controls, data handling procedures, staff permissions, incident response, and workflow boundaries for taxpayer information. The system is designed so AI agents assist with intake, organization, and draft outputs, but never replace required human review of tax data.

Encryption at rest and in transit: Client data is encrypted in transit using current secure transport standards and encrypted at rest using strong encryption practices, including AES-256 for stored data and backups where applicable. This helps protect data both during upload and while stored within approved systems.

No model training on client data: Client files, messages, and extracted fields are never used to train public AI models. Data is processed only for the requested workflow. This gives firms the efficiency of AI without exposing confidential tax records for broader model learning.

Access controls and auditability: Access is restricted by role so only authorized staff can view or approve taxpayer information. We can structure workflows so front-desk staff, preparers, reviewers, and owners each see only the level of detail they need. Audit logs can track uploads, reviews, approvals, and workflow status changes.

US-based data residency: For firms concerned about where taxpayer data is stored and processed, we configure deployments to use US-based infrastructure only.

PII handling and masking: Sensitive identifiers such as Social Security numbers and Employer Identification Numbers are masked in logs, internal summaries, and support views wherever possible. We minimize unnecessary exposure of full identifiers and limit where raw PII appears in the workflow.

Human oversight on sensitive actions: No return is filed, no bookkeeping entry is posted, and no sensitive exception is resolved without human review. The system is designed for recommendation, draft generation, and workflow acceleration, not autonomous decision-making on taxpayer matters.

Incident response and breach protocol: We support documented breach notification and response procedures, including escalation paths, containment steps, audit review, and communication planning. If your firm already has an incident response process, the AI workflow is mapped into that process rather than operating outside it.

Business Associate Agreement if needed: While tax practices are generally governed by tax data security rules rather than HIPAA, we can provide a Business Associate Agreement if your client mix or workflow requires it, such as cases involving health-related financial records or adjacent regulated services.

The result is a system that improves speed and consistency without compromising the control standards your profession requires.

Implementation Timeline

We recommend a four-week rollout designed to minimize disruption and help your staff build confidence before full deployment.

Week 1: Discovery call and workflow mapping
We document your current intake, bookkeeping, communication, and review workflows. We identify your highest-friction tasks, approval checkpoints, software stack, and security requirements.

Week 2: Agent build and testing with sample data
We configure the initial agents, map required fields and document types, and test the workflow using sample or sanitized data. This is where we validate reminders, extraction logic, categorization suggestions, and routing rules.

Week 3: Staff training and soft launch
We train your team on how to review outputs, approve or correct suggestions, and manage exceptions. The agents are then rolled out in a limited production setting, typically with one workflow or one group of clients first.

Week 4: Full deployment and optimization
Once the process is validated, we expand usage and fine-tune the system based on real-world patterns. This can include adjusting reminder timing, changing thresholds for anomaly detection, or improving intake logic for your most common return types.
